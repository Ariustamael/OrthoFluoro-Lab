# Medical limitations

OrthoFluoro Lab is an educational geometric visualisation. It is not a medical
device, diagnostic viewer, surgical navigation system, exposure simulator, or
patient-specific planning tool.

The current model omits real anatomical variation, pathology, tissue
attenuation, beam spectrum, scatter, noise physics, automatic exposure control,
distortion, calibration error, table and equipment collisions, device-specific
limits, sterility constraints, and radiation dose. The procedural object is
not a representation of a patient and the synthetic detector image is not a
clinically accurate radiograph.

Angles and distances describe the application’s own documented coordinate
system. They must not be copied into patient care or used to select, confirm, or
judge a real procedure. Any future clinical teaching material requires expert
review, source documentation, governance, privacy assessment, and validation
outside this prototype.
